# Personvern og databeskyttelse

## Hva samler vi inn?
- **Familiedata**: Navn, roller, medlemskap
- **Arrangementer**: Titler, beskrivelser, datoer, adresser
- **Helse**: Medisiner, timer, vaksiner, allergier, vekstdata
- **Kjæledyr**: Navn, type, veterinærbesøk, medisiner, forsikring
- **Handlelister**: Innkjøpslister og varer
- **Chat**: Meldinger mellom familiemedlemmer
- **Bursdager**: Navn og fødselsdatoer
- **Profiler**: E-post, telefonnummer, visningsnavn

## Hvordan bruker vi dataene?
- Familiekoordinering (arrangementer, handlelister, reiser)
- Påminnelser (varsler til telefonen)
- AI-funksjoner (oppskriftsforslag, destinasjonstips)
- Tredjepartstjenester (Spond-integrasjon)

## Lagring
- Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud)
- Firebase Storage (bilder, dokumenter)
- Alle data er kryptert under overføring (TLS)
- HTTPS er påtvunget av Firebase Hosting

## Tredjepartstjenester
- **Spond**: Laghåndtering (krever pålogging)
- **OpenAI**: AI-funksjoner (oppskrifter, destinasjonstips)
- **Google Maps**: Kart, geokoding, stedsforslag
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging**: Push-varsler

# Privacy and Data Protection

## What we collect?
- **Family data**: Names, roles, membership
- **Events**: Titles, descriptions, dates, locations
- **Health**: Medications, appointments, vaccinations, allergies, growth data
- **Pets**: Names, types, vet visits, medications, insurance
- **Shopping lists**: Items and quantities
- **Chat**: Messages between family members
- **Birthdays**: Names and birth dates
- **Profiles**: Email, phone number, display name

## How do we use the data?
- Family coordination (events, shopping lists, trips)
- Reminders (push notifications to phone)
- AI features (recipe suggestions, destination tips)
- Third-party integrations (Spond integration)

## Storage
- Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud)
- Firebase Storage (images, documents)
- All data encrypted in transit (TLS)
- HTTPS enforced by Firebase Hosting

## Third-party services
- **Spond**: Team management (requires login)
- **OpenAI**: AI features (recipes, destination tips)
- **Google Maps**: Maps, geocoding, place suggestions
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging**: Push notifications

## Your rights
- **Access**: You can see all data we have about you and your family
- **Deletion**: You can delete your account and all data
- **Portability**: You can export data in machine-readable format
- **Consent**: You can withdraw consent at any time
