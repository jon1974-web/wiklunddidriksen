1. Introduction

Familiesenter is a family app that helps families organize their daily lives. We take your privacy seriously and this policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data Controller

Jon Wiklund Didriksen
jon@wiklunddidriksen.com

3. Data We Collect

Account and Profile

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)

Family

  • Family name
  • Family roles (owner, admin, member)
  • Invite codes

Health

  • Medications, appointments, vaccinations, allergies, growth
  • This data is specially protected under GDPR Article 9

School and Kindergarten

  • Children's names, school/kindergarten information and photos
  • Contacts (teachers, classmates, parents)
  • Schedules

Travel

  • Trips, transport, accommodation, restaurants, activities

Chat

  • Messages, images, reactions

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Operate and improve the app
  • Provide you access to family data
  • Send appointment reminders
  • AI features (recipes, voice-to-event, photo-to-event)
  • Sync with third-party services (Spond, Google Calendar)

6. Storage and Security

  • AES-256 encryption for Spond passwords
  • Google-managed encryption for other data
  • TLS encryption in transit
  • Family-scoped Firestore rules
  • Daily automated backups (30-day retention)

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can see all data we have about you
  • Rectification: You can correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: You can delete your account
  • Data portability: You can export your data
  • Consent: You can withdraw consent at any time

8. Children's Data

The app collects data about children (names, school information, contacts). This data is subject to strict protection rules. Parents/guardians have full control over children's data.
